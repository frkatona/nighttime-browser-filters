Reading Sample

Black text on a white block for contrast and glare checks

This article card is intentionally bright. It gives you a clean white reading surface with solid black text so you can judge whether a method simply makes the page darker or actually makes it more comfortable to read at night.

Look at the body text, the headline, the small metadata line, and the amount of glare you feel around the edge of the white block. Simple dimming may reduce intensity while still feeling sharp. More tone-aware methods should reduce the hard edge between the white surface and the darker surroundings.

The gallery above gives you a second type of stressor: bright highlights, colored regions, and image detail. The combination of those images with this white text block should make it easier to compare which methods calm the page down without destroying readability.
